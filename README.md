## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Superstore retail dataset** to understand the company's sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, discount strategy, regional performance, and time-based business trends.

The project was developed as an end-to-end Data Analytics project using:

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- SQL
- Power BI

The primary objective was not only to calculate business metrics, but also to transform the raw data into **actionable business insights and recommendations**.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company generates a large amount of sales data across different:

- Customers
- Products
- Categories
- Sub-Categories
- Regions
- States
- Orders
- Shipping modes
- Discount levels
- Years and months

However, raw transactional data does not directly answer important management questions.

This project investigates questions such as:

1. How much revenue does the company generate?
2. How much profit does the company generate?
3. What is the overall profit margin?
4. Which categories generate the most sales?
5. Which categories generate the most profit?
6. Which products generate the highest revenue?
7. Which products generate the highest profit?
8. Which products and sub-categories generate losses?
9. How does discounting affect profitability?
10. Which regions perform best and worst?
11. How does business performance change over time?
12. Which years and months perform best?
13. Where should management investigate further?
14. What actions could potentially improve profitability?

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Perform exploratory data analysis.
- Understand the structure and quality of the dataset.
- Calculate important business KPIs.
- Analyze sales and profitability.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing categories.
- Identify profitable and loss-making sub-categories.
- Investigate product-level profitability.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Analyze regional performance.
- Analyze yearly and monthly trends.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Convert analytical findings into business recommendations.
